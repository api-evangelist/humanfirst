# HumanFirst (Atlas)

> HumanFirst is a digital health measurement company whose Atlas platform helps clinical research and life-sciences teams evaluate, select, and operationalize sensor-based digital health technologies (wearables, connected devices, and digital biomarkers) for clinical trials and real-world evidence. HumanFirst is now part of ICON plc's Digital Health Technologies (DHT) capabilities. The Atlas platform is delivered as an authenticated web application; the company does not currently publish a public developer API.

## Platform
- [Atlas login](https://app.gohumanfirst.com/login): Authenticated Atlas web application.
- [HumanFirst / ICON](https://www.gohumanfirst.com/): Company home; HumanFirst is part of ICON.
- [ICON Digital Health Technologies](https://www.iconplc.com/DHT): ICON DHT capabilities that Atlas is integrated into.

## Security & Compliance
- [Security overview](https://www.gohumanfirst.com/security/overview): Information Security Program aligned to SSAE SOC 2, guided by NIST CSF, GDPR-compliant.
